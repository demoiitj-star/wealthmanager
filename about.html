About

Building Wealth With Purpose

Wealth management begins with understanding what you have, what you need, what you want, what could go wrong, and how to build toward it with clarity and discipline.

My professional journey began in the insurance and financial-services space, where I have worked with 200–300 clients and helped them understand financial-protection solutions. That work taught me that strong financial planning does not begin with aggressive growth—it begins with protection, stability and understanding the risk behind every decision.

Today, I am expanding my knowledge across investment strategy, portfolio management, market research, technical analysis and financial risk management. Alongside my professional work, I am pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering, which helps me approach markets with a more analytical, data-oriented mindset.

My Core Belief

Protect wealth before attempting to grow it.

This principle shapes the way I think about strategy. Whether we are discussing risk planning, diversification, or long-term allocation, the starting point is always the same: make sure the financial foundation is strong enough to support the next step.
