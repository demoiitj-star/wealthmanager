Illustrative Case Studies

Educational Examples

These are hypothetical examples created to demonstrate how financial planning frameworks can be applied in real-world situations.

Young Professional

Objective: Build long-term wealth while maintaining adequate financial protection.

  • Emergency liquidity
  • Protection planning
  • Long-term investments
  • Diversification
  • Regular reviews

Family Wealth

Objective: Protect household income and create a structured long-term financial plan.

  • Life protection
  • Goal planning
  • Children's education
  • Retirement planning
  • Asset allocation

High-Risk Investor

Objective: Create a framework for an investor interested in equities and derivatives.

  • Position sizing
  • Risk limits
  • Diversification
  • Trading capital vs long-term capital
  • Drawdown management

The above examples are illustrative and not actual client records or outcomes. They exist to show how a structured discussion around goals, protection, risk, and allocation can take place.
