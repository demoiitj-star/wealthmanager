Building Wealth With Purpose

Wealth management is not simply about finding the next investment opportunity. It begins with understanding what you have, what you need, what you want, what could go wrong, and how to build toward it.

My professional journey began in the insurance and financial-services space, where I have worked with 200–300 clients and helped them understand financial-protection solutions. This experience introduced me to an important principle: A strong financial plan should protect wealth before attempting to grow it.
